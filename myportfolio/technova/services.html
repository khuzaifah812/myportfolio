FAQ

Before you start

Do you work with clients outside Uganda?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere, using video calls, shared project boards and weekly test links. Everything is written in English.

What time zone are you in?

We are in Kampala on East Africa Time (UTC+3), which overlaps with working hours across Africa, Europe, the Middle East and much of Asia.

How does a project start?

With a free discovery call. We then send a written proposal with the scope, timeline and cost, and start only once you approve it.

Can you work with the systems we already use?

Yes. We connect existing tools such as accounting software, Mobile Money, payment gateways, SMS and email, or migrate your data to a new system.

Do you work with large organizations?

Yes. We build for startups, small and medium businesses and large enterprises, including multi-branch systems with role-based access and audit trails.

What happens after launch?

We train your team and offer support plans covering monitoring, backups, security updates and a help desk you can reach by phone, email or WhatsApp.
