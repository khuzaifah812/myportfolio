Our story

Software that was planned before it was built.

Too many organizations pay for systems that were rushed from idea to launch with no shared plan in between. The result is software that half-fits and costs more to fix than it did to build.

TechNova works the other way round. We map the system with you first, agree the scope and cost in writing, then build in short cycles you can see and test.
